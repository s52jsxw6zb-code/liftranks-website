PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 24, 2025

Last Updated: January 24, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lift Ranks. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Full name
  • Profile information (optional: gender, location)

Fitness Data:

  • Exercise types and personal best records
  • Workout history and timestamps
  • Body weight (if provided)
  • Progress tracking data

Location Data:

  • Approximate location (city/region) for local leaderboards
  • We do not track precise GPS location

Usage Data:

  • App usage patterns and preferences
  • Device information (type, operating system)
  • Crash reports and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your rankings on leaderboards
  • To track your workout progress
  • To enable competition features (local and global leaderboards)
  • To award medals and achievements
  • To improve our app and develop new features
  • To send important updates about the service

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Public Information:

  • Your full name and personal best records are visible on public leaderboards
  • Your approximate location (city/region) may be displayed with your leaderboard ranking
  • Medal awards and achievements may be visible to other users

Private Leaderboards:

  • Information shared in custom friend leaderboards is only visible to members of that group

We Do Not Sell Your Data:

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Service Providers:

We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our app (hosting, analytics, customer support). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at [email protected]

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.

YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

Managing Your Privacy Settings

You have control over your privacy within Lift Ranks:

Profile Visibility

  • Public Profile: Your full name and personal bests appear on global and local leaderboards
  • Location Display: You can choose whether to display your city/region on leaderboards
  • Profile Information: Optional details (gender) can be hidden from public view

Leaderboard Participation

  • Global Leaderboards: Opt out in Settings > Privacy > Leaderboard Visibility
  • Local Leaderboards: Disable location-based rankings separately
  • Private Groups: You control which custom leaderboards you join

Data Collection Preferences

  • Usage Analytics: Opt out of analytics in Settings > Privacy > Analytics
  • Location Services: Disable in device settings (affects local leaderboards)
  • Notifications: Manage push notification preferences in app settings

Account Management

  • Delete Account: Permanently remove your account and data via Settings > Account > Delete Account

Marketing Communications

  • Email Updates: Unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time
  • Push Notifications: Manage in Settings > Notifications
  • In-App Messages: Customize which types of updates you receive

Do Not Sell My Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under CCPA. Contact us at [email protected] to learn more.

Contacting Us About Privacy

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@liftranks.com
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 48 hours
